### 1. Общие положения
Настоящая Политика конфиденциальности описывает, как приложение **TodoApp** («Приложение») обрабатывает и защищает информацию пользователей. Мы уважаем вашу конфиденциальность и собираем только минимально необходимые данные для работы функций Приложения.

### 3. Как используются данные
- Для предоставления основного функционала планировщика задач, календаря и таймера концентрации.

## English

### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how **TodoApp** ("the App", bundle ID `com.helltrilla.todoapp`) handles and protects user information. We respect your privacy and adhere to a strict data-minimization principle.

### 2. Data We Collect and Store
- **Tasks, Subtasks, and Categories**: All tasks, checklists, custom categories, and Pomodoro timer statistics you create are stored **locally on your device** (`SharedPreferences`). We do not access, collect, or transmit the contents of your tasks to any external servers.
- **Local Profile**: When using the offline "Internal Sign-In" mode, your display name and login credentials remain strictly on your device.
- **Email Address (Email OTP Sign-In)**: If you choose to sign in via Email OTP, your email address is transmitted securely over HTTPS to **Supabase Auth** solely to deliver a one-time 6-digit verification code and authenticate your session.

### 3. How We Use Your Data
- To provide core task management, calendar scheduling, and focus timer functionality.
- To authenticate your session via one-time passcode (OTP) when cloud sign-in is selected.
- We **do not** integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or sell user data to third parties.

### 4. Account & Data Deletion
In full compliance with Apple App Store and Google Play policies, you can **permanently delete your account and all associated data** at any time directly within the App:
1. Open the **Profile & Settings** screen.
2. Scroll down to the **Management (`Управление`)** section.
3. Tap **Delete Account & Data (`Удалить аккаунт и данные`)** and confirm.

You may also request deletion of your Supabase authentication record by contacting the developer at [helltrilla66@gmail.com](mailto:helltrilla66@gmail.com).
